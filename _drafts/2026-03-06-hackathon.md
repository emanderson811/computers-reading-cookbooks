In January 2026, I hosted a hackathon for library staff in celebration of the completion of digitization for this project and the addition of 201 new cookbooks to the [Iowa Cookbook Digital Collection](https://digitalcollections.lib.iastate.edu/cookbooks/). 

Participants attended a kick-off workshop on January 26 for an introduction to the dataset and several suggested text analysis tools including VoyantTools, AntConc, and a guided Python Jupyter notebook. They then explored the dataset asynchronously throughout the week and discussed findings in a Teams chat. Topics explored included searching for vegetarian and vegan recipes, identifying the most popular fruits, finding and quantifying name variations for scotcheroos and taverns, and more.
