03 / Behind the analysis

From cookbook
to corpus.

Computers help us notice patterns. Historical context helps us ask what those patterns mean.

About the project and its approach ↗

  1. Digitize

    Begin with cookbooks published by Iowa community organizations.

  2. Recognize the text

    Turn scanned pages into machine-readable text using optical character recognition.

  3. Prepare & compare

    Examine the text, account for errors, and explore words and similarities.

  4. Return to the recipes

    Read the sources closely. Interpret patterns in their community and historical contexts.
