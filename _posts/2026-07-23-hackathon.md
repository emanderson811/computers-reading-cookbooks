## Hackathon

In January 2026, I hosted a hackathon for library staff in celebration of the completion of digitization for this project and the addition of 201 new cookbooks to the [Iowa Cookbook Digital Collection](https://digitalcollections.lib.iastate.edu/cookbooks/). 

Participants attended a kick-off workshop on January 26 for an introduction to the dataset and several suggested text analysis tools including VoyantTools, AntConc, and a guided Python Jupyter notebook. They then explored the dataset asynchronously throughout the week and discussed findings in a Teams chat. Topics explored included searching for vegetarian and vegan recipes, identifying the most popular fruits, finding and quantifying name variations for scotcheroos and taverns, and more.

## Potluck

In partnership with the Library Staff Association, I hosted a library-wide potluck celebration. All members of the library were invited to explore the newly digitized cookbooks and choose a recipe to prepare and share with colleagues. Attendees contributed nearly 30 dishes to the potluck spread including: Popcorn Cake from the [1984 Eddyville Oregon Trail Days Cookbook](https://digitalcollections.lib.iastate.edu/cookbooks/items/cookbooks28985.html), Scandinavian Kringla from a [1982 Friends of the Octagon cookbook](https://digitalcollections.lib.iastate.edu/cookbooks/items/cookbooks36005.html), and an intriguing noodle dish called “Zimmy Zammy” from the [1977 Ruth Circle Cookbook from Nevada, IA](https://digitalcollections.lib.iastate.edu/cookbooks/items/cookbooks32285.html).

## Carleton Day of DH

In June, I attended Carleton College's [Day of Digital Humanities](https://www.carleton.edu/dayofdh/) where I presented a lighting talk and digital demonstration of the project. I also taught an afternoon workshop on exploring the Iowa Community Cookbook Corpus using Voyant Tools and AntConc.
